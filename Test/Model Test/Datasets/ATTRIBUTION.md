# Dataset Attribution and Licences

This folder redistributes third-party evaluation datasets under their original
licences. Each is credited below. No modifications were made to the audio
itself; where only a subset is included, that is stated.

**Places365 is deliberately absent** — its terms prohibit redistribution of its
images. See the README for how to obtain it and where to place it.

## Sound Classification — SONYC-UST v2 (subset)

A 664-clip subset of the validation split is included, with the matching
annotation rows in `annotations_test_split.csv`.

> Cartwright, M., Cramer, J., Mendez, A. E. M., Wang, Y., Wu, H., Lostanlen, V.,
> Fuentes, M., Dove, G., Mydlarz, C., Salamon, J., Nov, O., & Bello, J. P.
> (2020). *SONYC Urban Sound Tagging (SONYC-UST): A Multilabel Dataset from an
> Urban Acoustic Sensor Network.* Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3966543

**Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) —
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Voice Note Transcription — LibriSpeech `test-clean`

The complete `test-clean` split (2,620 utterances) with its transcript files.

> Panayotov, V., Chen, G., Povey, D., & Khudanpur, S. (2015). *Librispeech: An
> ASR corpus based on public domain audio books.* In 2015 IEEE International
> Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP), 5206–5210.
> https://doi.org/10.1109/ICASSP.2015.7178964

> of Emotional Speech and Song (RAVDESS): A dynamic, multimodal set of facial
> and vocal expressions in North American English.* PLoS ONE, 13(5), e0196391.
> https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0196391
